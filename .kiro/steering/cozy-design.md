# Cozy design rules

- Design for elderly and non-technical users first: use warm, literal copy, avoid jargon, and make the
  next action obvious.
- Cozy is the default. Use pastel purple, cream, lavender, warm orange, and strong text contrast.
- Custom body text is at least 18 pt and speech-bubble text is at least 19 pt. Custom action targets
  should be at least 44×44 pt where space permits and have meaningful VoiceOver labels.
- Cozy has no brightness flicker, lightning, jump scares, falling pumpkins, or sudden large motion.
- Every animation has a Reduce Motion alternative that communicates the same state with a fade or
  static pose.
- Motion must guide attention or communicate state. Do not add decoration-only animation.
- Warnings are caring and direct, never alarming or blaming.
